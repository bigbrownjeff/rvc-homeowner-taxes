The facts ledger is the source of truth for public claims. Any approved
public-facing outreach copy must be checked against the current ledger and live
site before it is published.

**How to apply:**
- On a material fact change, update the ledger first and propagate it through
  every deployed public surface.
- Re-verify volatile figures and public links on the day of publication rather
  than copying values into a durable checklist.
- Keep only reusable, public-safe templates in source control. Recipient
  research, direct-message drafts, routing, send state, and replies belong in
  an owner-authorized operational system.
- Clearly date historical public artifacts where their vintage matters; do not
  let a historical value silently stand in for the current claim.
