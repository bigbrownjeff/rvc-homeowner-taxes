A historical outreach phase was completed. Recipient identities, contact routes,
copy, timing, and response details have been removed from source control.

**Before any future contact:**
- Obtain owner approval and consult the authorized operational record rather
  than assuming someone has or has not been contacted.
- Re-verify current facts, representation, and public routes on the day of use.
- Keep any message voluntary, fact-based, and free of implied support.
- Store recipient-specific preparation, send logs, and reply handling outside
  the repository.
