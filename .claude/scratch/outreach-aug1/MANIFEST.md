# Public outreach guidance

This directory contains only public-safe channel material. It deliberately does
not keep individual or organization names, contact details, routing
instructions, mailing-list information, send or reply status, mailbox IDs, or
research about private residents.

## Publishing method

1. Treat the live site as the source of truth. Re-verify facts, numbers,
   proposal details, and links on the day of publication.
2. Keep wording voluntary, nonpartisan, and fact-based. Do not imply support,
   affiliation, endorsement, or consent.
3. Use one approved public message at a time, with a channel-specific tracking
   URL only where it has been approved.
4. Recheck links and the social card after deployment. Record aggregate
   campaign results only in an authorized operational system.

## Public-safe template

```text
[Plain-language, voluntary framing]
[What a visitor can learn or do]
[Verified public URL]
```

## Local operational work

Recipient-specific preparation, send logs, and reply handling stay local in
`recipient-drafts/`, which is repository-ignored. Do not force-add those files
or put contact details in this directory. Historical operational records belong
in authorized systems, not source control.
